Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

Information you provide

The contact form may collect your name, email address, selected topic, and message. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, Social Security numbers, account numbers, medical records, or other sensitive information.

Technical information

Hosting and security systems may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, request time, requested page, and error or security events.

How information is used

  • Respond to requests and provide requested support.
  • Operate, secure, and improve the website.
  • Maintain records needed for transactions, security, or dispute resolution.

Payments and external services

Payment providers, Amazon, and other linked services process information under their own privacy policies. Full payment-card credentials should not be submitted through the public contact form.

Sharing

Dominion Healing does not sell contact-form information. Information may be shared with service providers that support website operation, payment, delivery, security, or legal compliance.

Retention and requests

Information is retained only as reasonably needed for support, operations, records, security, and legal obligations. Use the contact form to request access, correction, or deletion, subject to verification and applicable retention requirements.

Children

This website is not directed to children for independent commercial transactions. A parent or legal guardian should contact Dominion Healing regarding a child's information.
