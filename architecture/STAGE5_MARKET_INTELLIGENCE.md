# Stage 5 — External-Estimated Market Intelligence

## Purpose

Stage 5 adds a governed path for Similarweb-style exports and other approved third-party estimates. It turns those inputs into market signals and a draft storefront growth-intelligence audit without representing estimates as first-party truth.

## Evidence boundary

- `SYNTHETIC_TEST`: testing only
- `EXTERNAL_ESTIMATED`: market and competitor signals only
- `FIRST_PARTY_VERIFIED`: operational evidence, still subject to human review
- `PARTNER_GOVERNED`: future contract-limited collaboration

External estimates may support benchmarking, hypotheses, and scenario design. They may not verify orders, revenue, conversion, margin, customer identity, or campaign causation.

## Cash-flow product

The first product template is the **AI Storefront and Growth Intelligence Audit**. It produces a competitive signal summary, storefront friction hypotheses, measurement gaps, and a prioritized thirty-day test backlog.

Pricing, publication, prospect contact, client-data acceptance, and final delivery remain human-gated. The committed fixture is not an actual Similarweb export and must not be described as real traffic or business performance.

## Data commercialization rule

Dominion monetizes governed analysis, aggregate insights, measurement systems, and read-only micro-builds. Raw identifiable data and health-sensitive advertising data are not products.

## Runtime boundary

Stage 5 remains `shadow_only`. No customer contact, publishing, campaign activation, money movement, live trading, production deployment, registry mutation, agent promotion, Obsidian runtime, or Browser Agent startup is authorized.
