# Stage 7 — Revenue Vertical Activation Structure

Stage 7 converts Dominion's governance and intelligence foundation into a machine-readable revenue portfolio without authorizing live publication, prospect contact, pricing, spending, or production activation.

## Wave 1

Primary revenue verticals:

1. `commerce_fulfillment` — DominionHealing Wix Store
2. `kdp_publishing` — owned books and educational publishing

Shadow support:

3. `analytics_services` — market intelligence, measurement, and future paid audits

The wave limit is two primary revenue verticals. This avoids fragmenting operator attention and evidence across the full portfolio.

## Current truth state

- The Wix Store is not fully launch-ready because isolation, repair, checkout, fulfillment, and unit-economics evidence remain incomplete.
- No canonical KDP title inventory or title-level rights/proof records exist in the repository.
- The analytics product is drafted, but no actual Similarweb export, approved pilot scope, or price exists.
- A large revenue outcome is a strategic target, not a forecast.
- No Stage 7 file authorizes an external action.

## Activation model

Each vertical has:

- a commercial model
- a value offer
- current blockers
- launch gates
- required evidence
- success metrics
- a first-cash definition
- human approvals
- prohibited actions
- explicit execution and revenue truth fields

The Human Overseer approves the wave and each consequential external action. The control plane verifies evidence and prevents unsupported transitions.

## Parallel work boundary

Wix remediation and KDP title preparation may proceed in parallel because they are the two approved Wave 1 primary verticals. Analytics may process permitted evidence and produce drafts in shadow mode.

Digital products, lead generation, content distribution, surplus, and trading do not become new primary revenue work until Wave 1 evidence supports expansion or the Human Overseer records a deliberate reprioritization.

## Completion

Stage 7 is structurally complete when the manifesto, vertical contracts, registry priorities, runtime gates, schemas, tests, and CI validation are canonical.

Wave 1 is commercially complete only after real exit-gate evidence exists. Repository structure alone is not revenue.
