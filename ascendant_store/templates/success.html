What happens now

  1. Receipt email — sent by Stripe immediately, from a stripe.com address. It carries your order reference.
  2. Delivery email — sent separately by Ascendant Digital with your download link. Usually within a few minutes.

If the delivery email has not arrived

Check the spam or promotions folder first — automated delivery mail lands there more often than it should.

If it is not there after 30 minutes, reply directly to your Stripe receipt or email us with the order reference from it. We will resend the link by hand. You will not need to pay again.
