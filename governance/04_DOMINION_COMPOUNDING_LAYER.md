# DOMINION COMPOUNDING LAYER — AMENDMENT 03
*What must exist BEFORE gate one clears, so the system multiplies instead of restarting.*
*Status: DRAFT — Founder GO required. Companion to Amendments 01 and 02.*

---

## §0 — THE PRINCIPLE
Most operations clear their first revenue gate and then stall, because everything they built was single-use. The next lane starts from zero. The advantage evaporates.

Compounding requires that the work done to clear gate one becomes **reusable input** to gates two through six. Eight assets below must be built in Tier B *now*, while VoltEdge runs the sprint, so that the moment `REVENUE: PROVEN` lands, the system expands at machine pace rather than rebuilding.

**Test for every asset here:** does it get *more* valuable the more lanes run? If no, it is operations, not compounding — build it later.

---

## §1 — THE REPLICATION KIT (largest single multiplier)
The sprint that clears VoltEdge must be captured as an executable playbook, not a memory. Required artifacts, produced *during* the sprint, not after:

- **LANE_PLAYBOOK.md** — the exact sequence that produced the first dollar, generalized: economics verification → offer validation → channel test → fulfillment proof → reconciliation → gate.
- **Decision log** — every judgment call, what was chosen, why, and what it cost. This is where the real IP lives.
- **Reusable components** — the SKU economics calculator, the acceptance-test script, the reconciliation query, the creative brief template, the review-queue checklist. Parameterized, not hardcoded to VoltEdge.
- **Failure register** — everything that broke and the fix. Prevents lane two paying the same tuition.

**Target:** lane two reaches its first transaction in materially less time than lane one, and the delta is measured and reported. If lane two takes as long as lane one, the kit failed and gets rebuilt before lane three.

## §2 — APPRECIATING ASSETS (build during the sprint, not after)
Assets that gain value with time and volume. Every one of these is cheap now and expensive to start late.

1. **Owned email list** — capture live on every property from day one, even pre-launch. The only channel no platform can revoke.
2. **Content library** — every asset published stays published and keeps working. Compounding requires volume plus time; starting late cannot be bought back.
3. **Domain authority** — technical SEO foundation, schema, site architecture, internal linking. Slow to earn, permanent once earned.
4. **Social proof** — a systematic post-purchase review and testimonial request from the very first order. The first ten reviews are the hardest and the most valuable.
5. **First-party data** — what buyers respond to, by segment and channel. Under Healing Memory, this is institutional knowledge, not analytics exhaust.

**Ruling:** these are funded from float (Amendment 02 §4) and are exempt from the single-lane Tier C restriction — they are Tier B, unlimited parallel, all lanes.

## §3 — THE LEARNING LOOP (the actual AI advantage)
An AI-operated business's structural edge over a human-operated one is loop speed. Without an explicit loop, agents produce output without learning; with one, every cycle raises the baseline.

Required loop, running weekly:
`measure → attribute → identify the single largest constraint → change one thing → measure again → write the result to Healing Memory`

- One variable per cycle. Multi-variable changes produce unattributable results and are prohibited in paid channels.
- Every result — win or loss — is written to a persistent record queryable by every agent. A loss that isn't recorded gets repeated in the next lane.
- The weekly report (Amendment 01 §5) carries: what changed, what moved, what was learned, what changes next.
- **No fabricated causation.** Correlation is INFERRED, not VERIFIED.

---

## §4 — THE PROOF ENGINE (feeds the standard directly)
Amendment 02's entire path depends on a credible reference implementation. Credibility is an artifact, and it is produced during the work or not at all.

- **Evidence archive:** every transaction ID, deploy hash, health check, audit result, correction, and incident — retained, timestamped, append-only.
- **Public correction record:** documented failures and fixes. Counterintuitive but decisive — published corrections are what make a governance standard believable. A perfect record reads as fabricated.
- **Case study artifact:** the metrics and narrative of gate one, drafted as it happens. This becomes Stage 2 collateral in Amendment 02 §6.
- **No external publication of any of it** until Stage 1 clears. Built now, released on gate.

---

## §5 — DEFENSIBILITY (what stops the copy)
Once the standard is public, the mechanism is visible. Four defenses, ranked by durability:

1. **The evidence record** — an unforgeable operating history. A copyist can take the spec on day one; they cannot take the proof.
2. **Proprietary IP held back** — PhiMemory and internal agent implementations stay outside the open spec (Amendment 02 §6 Stage 2). Open the *what*; hold the *how*.
3. **Data flywheel** — accumulated first-party learning across lanes. Grows non-linearly with lane count.
4. **Trust structure** — Dominion Legacy Trust as the durable IP and royalty holder. **UNKNOWN:** whether current filings and IP assignments are complete for licensing at Stage 4. Requires qualified legal review, not agent assertion.

## §6 — REINVESTMENT RULE
The first dollars are the highest-leverage dollars in the system's life. Default allocation of verified net revenue, until amended:
- **50%** into the channel that produced it (proven CAC only — never a channel with UNKNOWN economics)
- **30%** into appreciating assets (§2)
- **20%** reserve — refunds, disputes, tax, and shock absorption

**Prohibited:** funding a second Tier C lane from the first lane's revenue until the first lane holds `REVENUE: PROVEN` for 90 days. Premature diversification is the documented failure mode of every ambitious multi-lane operation.

---

## §7 — TIER C HANDOFF STAGING
The single-lane Tier C restriction only slows things down if the next lane is unprepared. It won't be.

While VoltEdge holds Tier C, every other lane advances to **Tier C-Ready** in Tier B: economics verified, offers validated, creative produced and review-approved, channel accounts authorized, acceptance tests written, gates identified. Everything short of the irreversible action.

**Result:** the handoff is a GO decision, not a project start. Queue order — Dominion Healing (digital, float-perfect, fastest) → Publishing/KDP → Surplus → Trading. Founder may reorder.

---

## §8 — FAILURE CONTAINMENT (scale multiplies mistakes too)
Compounding is directionless — it amplifies errors at the same rate as wins.
- **Blast radius limits:** every automated action declares its maximum reversible impact before it runs.
- **Kill switches:** every channel, every agent, every automation — one command to stop, tested before launch, not after an incident.
- **Anomaly halt:** spend, refund rate, dispute rate, or error rate outside declared bounds triggers automatic pause and a report. No agent may resume without Founder GO.
- **The Buddy rule:** any agent found asserting unverified completion is suspended from consequential actions immediately, without debate.

---

## §9 — WHAT THIS COSTS
Honest accounting: §1 through §8 add roughly 20–30% effort to the sprint and delay nothing on the critical path, because all of it is Tier B work running parallel to Tier C.

The trade: a slightly heavier sprint in exchange for lane two moving at a fraction of lane one's cost, and a proof archive that makes the standard sellable. Skipping this layer is what makes an operation stall at lane one — and stalling at lane one is what ends the larger goal.

**Founder GO required on:** the reinvestment rule (§6), the Tier C queue order (§7), and blast-radius/kill-switch requirements before any automated channel launches (§8).
