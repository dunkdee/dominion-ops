## §0 — THE CONSTRAINT, STATED PLAINLY
Operating capital: $0. This is not fatal — several of the companies in Amendment 02 §1 started near zero — but it changes the plan materially and it changes *which lane goes first.* Anyone who tells you the plan is unaffected is not being straight with you.

**What $0 removes:** all paid acquisition, paid tooling, legal review, bookkeeping software, anything with a monthly fee. Decision #5 on the sheet is **withdrawn** until capital exists.

**What $0 does not remove:** everything that costs time instead of money — and at zero capital, **time is the currency.** The entire plan is now denominated in it.

**Hard rule, non-waivable:** no debt, no credit, no borrowing against necessities, no funds needed for living expenses. A business that requires risking the founder's stability to start is not a business — it is a wager. Dominion's own law forbids operating in disorder. This is that rule applied to money.

## §1 — THE SEQUENCING CHANGES (honest revision)
Amendment 01 named VoltEdge as Tier C holder. **At $0, that ranking is wrong** and I am correcting it rather than defending a prior document.

VoltEdge is a physical-goods store. It needs traffic; traffic costs money or time; and each sale carries supplier cost, shipping, and refund exposure. It is the *slowest* path to a verified dollar at zero capital.

**Revised Tier C holder: PUBLISHING / DIGITAL.** Reasoning:
- The KDP titles already exist and are already listed — inventory paid for in the past, sellable today at zero marginal cost.
- Digital products carry no COGS, no supplier, no shipping, no fulfillment failure mode. Amendment 02 §4 already called them float-perfect.
- The Revenue Proof Gate's five criteria are far easier to satisfy with a digital delivery than a dropshipped physical order.
- A digital sale generates the same credential — a verified dollar with evidence — for Amendment 02 Stage 1.

**VoltEdge drops to TIER B**: economics table, storefront truth, variant-audit repair, organic content. Fully C-READY, activating the moment capital or proven organic traffic exists.

## §2 — THE ZERO-COST ACQUISITION STACK
Ranked by dollar-per-effort at zero spend. Every item costs only time.

1. **Existing published assets** — the live KDP titles. Optimize what already exists before creating anything new: keyword and category audit, description rewrite, cover assessment, series linkage, pricing test. Amazon supplies the traffic.
2. **Short-form video** — the only channel where zero budget still reaches strangers at scale. TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Reels via authorized accounts only. Meta stays RESTRICTED_HOLD. Volume and consistency beat production value.
3. **SEO on owned properties** — slow, compounding, free. Start now precisely because it takes months.
4. **Marketplace surface area** — every platform where listing is free and the platform brings the buyer.
5. **Owned email list** — free to start, and the only audience no platform can revoke.
6. **Communities** — genuine participation where the audience already gathers. Contribution first; promotion is what gets you removed.

**Prohibited at $0:** any paid channel, any "growth" tool with a fee, any service promising traffic for payment.

## §4 — THE REVISED PATH TO DOLLAR ONE
No paid step. Every step is time.

**Days 1–3:** Full audit of existing KDP assets — every title, listing state, keyword and category placement, pricing, cover, description, royalty setting. Truth state per row. This is inventory you already own and have never optimized.
**Days 4–7:** Optimize listings. Stand up the $47 toolkit in Stripe and deactivate the $147 product (already an open action). Verify the complete purchase-to-delivery path end to end.
**Days 8–14:** Short-form content at volume, pointed at the strongest existing asset. One variable tested per cycle (Amendment 03 §3). Email capture live on every property.
**Ongoing:** SEO foundation, review requests from every buyer, replication kit written as it happens.

**Gate:** the first verified digital sale — transaction ID, delivery confirmed, payout reconciled, positive margin (trivially true for digital), repeated once. That clears Amendment 01 §2 and unlocks everything downstream.
