## PART A — CAPITAL FORMATION

### §1 — THE PREMISE, CORRECTED
The stated target is funding "without credit checks or paperwork — we just need documents in order." Research does not support that combination existing as described, and building a plan on it would fail at the application.

What the market actually offers a pre-revenue business:
- Traditional bank lending is largely unavailable to founders not yet generating income, because a startup with no revenue provides none of the signals — financial statements, tax returns, cash flow history, business credit profile — that banks use to assess risk.
- Lenders who skip revenue requirements substitute **personal credit** instead. A personal score of 680+ opens most doors; 720+ reaches the best terms. Business credit cards are among the most accessible capital for new businesses and are issued on the owner's personal credit score and total income, not business income.
- Lenders who skip **both** credit and revenue checks are, in practice, merchant cash advance providers. MCAs advance a lump sum repaid as a percentage of daily card sales, and are underwritten on projected sales rather than existing revenue. At $0 revenue there is nothing to repay from. **This is the predatory zone and it is prohibited under Amendment 06 §0.**

**Ruling:** no "no-check, no-paperwork" funding is pursued. It does not exist in a form that is safe at zero revenue.

### §2 — WHAT IS ACTUALLY AVAILABLE AT $0 (ranked, honest)
1. **Kiva** — the strongest fit by a wide margin. A microlender allowing U.S. small businesses to borrow up to $15,000 at 0% with no fees, funded by lenders contributing as little as $25 who are motivated to help rather than to profit. No credit requirement of the conventional kind. It is earned through your network and your story — which is exactly what you have and exactly what a credit file cannot measure.
2. **Vendor net-30 terms** — suppliers let you buy now and pay in 30 days; this provides no upfront cash but improves cash flow. Free, and it builds a business credit file for later.
3. **Reward crowdfunding** — crowdfunding typically does not require the good credit scores, strong revenues, or two years in business that loans demand, and credit checks are unusual. Requires an existing audience, which is why Amendment 06's content work is also capital work.
4. **Revenue itself** — the fastest legitimate capital available to you, and the one that improves every option above.

**Deliberately excluded:** MCAs, high-APR cards carried past intro periods, anything requiring a personal guarantee against assets you need, any broker charging fees to "get you approved."

### §3 — WHAT MAKES DOMINION FUNDING-READY (the real checklist)
None of this requires capital. All of it is Juris-assistable and most is free or near-free.
- Entity formation verified and in good standing; registered agent current
- **EIN** — free from the IRS, and required by essentially every funder
- Business bank account separate from personal (Amendment 04 §1)
- **D-U-N-S number** — free, opens the business credit file
- Operating agreement / trust documentation organized and current
- Licenses and registrations verified for FL and for each lane
- Clean financial records from the first dollar — the spreadsheet ledger counts
- A written business plan with realistic projections labeled as projections

**Ordering principle:** these are what convert a *story* into a *file*. Do them now, at zero cost, while revenue builds.

### §4 — JURIS: TRUE SCOPE (binding)
**Juris may:** organize and inventory existing legal documents; identify what is missing against a checklist; draft first versions of policies, agreements, and applications; research statutes and requirements with citations; track filing deadlines; prepare application packets; flag inconsistencies between documents.

**Juris may not:** confer legal validity on anything; give legal advice; file, sign, or submit; determine entity or trust structure; represent Dominion; or certify that documents are "legit." Dominion's own law already states agents are not counsel and that legal uncertainty resolves to `LEGAL_STATUS: UNRESOLVED / ACTION: HOLD`.

**The absolute line:** every document submitted to a funder must accurately represent Dominion's actual state. Projections are labeled as projections. Revenue is stated as $0 while it is $0. Overstating revenue, operations, or assets to obtain funding is fraud, not strategy, and no agent under this governance will produce it. Accurate documents, well organized, are genuinely persuasive — that is the whole game and it is winnable honestly.
