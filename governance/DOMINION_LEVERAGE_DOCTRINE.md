## §0 — THE STATED END STATE
Dominion's governed AI operating system becomes the reference standard for how enterprises run AI-operated business functions: the road map, the instruction set, the compliance guideline. Adopted widely, embedded deeply, visible to those who look — **the key that is hidden but seen.**

This is a legitimate position and it has precedents. It also has a precise mechanism, and it is not "build something good and wait."

## §1 — THE PRECEDENT PATTERN (how "hidden but seen" is actually achieved)
Every infrastructure standard that won did so the same way — none of them by being noticed first.

| Standard | What was hidden | What was seen | Value capture |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intel | The silicon inside the box | "Intel Inside" badge | Component margin + brand tax |
| ARM | The instruction set inside every phone | Licensee compliance | Per-unit royalty, forever |
| CUDA | The dev layer under every AI model | Ecosystem lock | Hardware nobody can substitute |
| Stripe | Seven lines of payment code | Developer trust | Percentage of every transaction |
| Linux/K8s | The base layer, free | The certification and support tier | Services, certification, enterprise SKU |

**The extracted mechanism, in order:**
1. **Reference implementation with proof** — the standard ran a real operation profitably before anyone was asked to adopt it. Nobody adopts an unproven standard, however elegant.
2. **Free at the base** — the specification, the constitution, the contracts are open. Adoption cost near zero.
3. **Value captured one layer up** — certification, compliance attestation, tooling, support, hosted runtime, royalty. Never at the spec layer.
4. **A timing wedge** — an external pressure that makes adoption urgent rather than optional.
5. **Named artifacts** — a standard people can cite by name gets adopted; a philosophy does not.

Dominion currently has #5 in draft, part of #2, and **none of #1**. That is the honest gap.

## §3 — THE FIVE MECHANISMS, STACKED (not blended)
The five leverage models are unlocked in sequence, each funded by the one before. Blending them simultaneously produces contradiction; stacking them produces compounding.

**Layer 1 — Amazon: float.** Customer pays before supplier is paid. Dominion already has this structurally via dropshipping and digital products. Formalize it (§4). *Unlock: first verified dollar (Amendment 01 §2).*

**Layer 2 — Walmart: cost discipline.** Margin protected by ruthless unit economics, not by price increases. Enforced by Amendment 01 §4. *Unlock: three months of verified positive contribution margin.*

**Layer 3 — Google: free distribution.** Give the base layer away to own the channel. Applies to both content and the governance spec itself. *Unlock: a working acquisition channel with known CAC.*

**Layer 4 — Tesla: own the customer.** Direct relationship, no intermediary owning the demand. Owned email list, owned storefront, owned audience. *Unlock: repeatable acquisition.*

**Layer 5 — Nvidia/AWS: productize the internal system.** The operating layer that ran the business becomes the product others build on. *Unlock: §6 gates.*

**The Dominion difference:** those companies took roughly a decade per layer because humans built each one. A governed AI operating system can advance several layers concurrently under Amendment 01's tier rules — parallel preparation, single-lane irreversible commitment. **The innovation is not a new business model. It is the compounding rate across proven ones.**

## §4 — FLOAT MECHANICS (Layer 1, formalized)
Negative working capital is Dominion's most underused existing asset.
- Measure and report the **cash conversion cycle** per lane: days from customer payment received to supplier obligation settled.
- Every new offer is evaluated on float contribution, not revenue alone.
- Digital products (KDP, toolkits, the $47 Credit Dispute Toolkit) are float-perfect: paid upfront, near-zero marginal cost, no supplier lag. **Ruling: digital offers are prioritized over physical for cash generation, physical for market position.**
- Float is working capital, not profit. It funds Layer 2 and 3 only. It never funds Tier C expansion in a second lane and is never reported as earnings.

Every company in §1 grew on resources they did not own. Dominion's available OPR, ranked by leverage per unit of effort:

**Rule:** before any build, the agent must state which OPR was evaluated and why building was chosen over renting. Unjustified building is a governance finding.

## §6 — PRODUCTIZATION PATH (Layer 5 — the actual end state)
Four stages. Each gate is non-waivable; no stage may be skipped or marketed ahead of.

**Stage 1 — REFERENCE IMPLEMENTATION.** Dominion runs its own lanes profitably under the standard.
*Gate:* one lane at `REVENUE: PROVEN` (Amendment 01 §2) with ≥90 days of evidence-backed operation and a published incident/correction record.
*Until this gate clears, no external adoption claim of any kind may be made.*

**Stage 2 — PUBLISHED SPECIFICATION.** The Dominion Standard released free and openly: constitution, truth states, agent lifecycle, agent contract, production-mutation sequence, evidence requirements. Named, versioned, citable. Free is the strategy, not generosity — it is the adoption mechanism.
*Gate:* Stage 1 cleared; legal review of trust/IP position; PhiMemory and other proprietary IP explicitly held back from the open spec.

**Stage 3 — CERTIFICATION AND ATTESTATION.** Value capture begins one layer above the spec: conformance assessment, audit artifacts, an implementation reference, a "Governed by Dominion Standard" mark.
*Gate:* ≥3 external implementations not owned by Dominion; qualified legal/compliance review of any attestation language.

**Stage 4 — HOSTED RUNTIME / LICENSING.** The governed operating layer itself licensed to enterprise, with perpetual royalty under the Trust — the long-term end state already recorded in Dominion's own documents.
*Gate:* Stage 3 demand proven; security and multi-tenancy review; Founder GO.

## §7 — WHAT KILLS THIS (name it to avoid it)
1. **Marketing the standard before the reference implementation exists.** Fatal to credibility, and unrecoverable. This is the single largest risk and the reason Stage 1 is gated hardest.
2. **Blending the five layers instead of stacking them.** Produces a company that is premium and discount, open and closed, simultaneously.
3. **Capturing value at the spec layer.** Charge for the standard and adoption stops. Every precedent in §1 gave the base away.
4. **Compliance claims that outrun verification.** Naming a regulation Dominion has not been tested against converts the wedge into liability.
5. **Building where renting was available.** Burns the runway the float was meant to fund.
6. **Agent capacity outrunning revenue capacity.** Already ruled on: Amendment 01 §6.
