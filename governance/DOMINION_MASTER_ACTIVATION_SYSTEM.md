## §0 — THREE ELEMENTS STILL MISSING
Amendments 01–04 close the strategy, revenue, compounding, and operations gaps. Three remain, and two of them cannot be closed by agents — they require Founder input or external verification.

**MISSING 1 — Capital reality. UNKNOWN.**

Without it, the sequencing may be right and unaffordable. **Required from Founder:** available operating capital, monthly fixed cost, and the maximum acceptable loss before the first dollar. **MISSING 2 — Demand validation. UNVERIFIED.**
No evidence exists that anyone wants these specific SKUs at these prices, or the $47 toolkit at that price. Unit economics prove a sale *would* be profitable; they do not prove a sale *will* happen. The 14-day sprint is, in truth, a demand test — and a null result is a legitimate outcome that saves months. Held as a stated hypothesis, not an assumption.

Agents can run at machine pace; approvals cannot. **The system's throughput ceiling is not compute — it is Founder attention.** Unaddressed, this becomes the exact bottleneck that stalls everything, and it stalls it quietly.

## §2 — DECISION BANDWIDTH PROTOCOL (closes MISSING 3)
Preserves human-final-authority absolutely while removing the founder as the rate limiter. Authority is not delegated — it is **pre-issued with bounds.**

**Standing authorizations** — the Founder authorizes a *class* of action in advance, with an explicit ceiling. The agent executes within bounds and reports; it does not re-ask. Any excursion beyond the bound halts and requires a fresh GO.
Examples of bounded classes:
- Read-only audits, diagnostics, and reporting — standing, unlimited
- Reversible Tier B work within an approved lane — standing
- Paid spend up to a stated daily cap on an approved channel — standing until cap or kill threshold
- Content generation and review-queue submission — standing; publication remains gated

**Always requires fresh individual GO — never standing:**
live money, trading transition, claimant contact, filings, signatures, external publication to a new channel, new vendor contracts, production mutation, lifting FROZEN, Tier C handoff, anything touching the Trust.

**Batching:** decisions are presented in one consolidated sheet per cycle, each with recommendation, evidence, cost of yes, cost of no, and reversibility. Not scattered across sessions.

**Escalation rule:** if a decision blocks a lane for more than one cycle, it is re-presented flagged as blocking, with the cost of continued delay stated.

## §3 — WHAT ACTIVATES WHAT (the loop that makes it self-expanding)
`gate cleared → replication kit updated (03 §1) → next C-READY lane activates → learning written to memory (03 §3) → reinvestment allocated (03 §6) → next lane clears faster → repeat`

Each cycle should complete faster than the last. **If cycle two is not measurably faster than cycle one, the replication kit failed and gets rebuilt before cycle three.** That measurement is the honest proof the system compounds — not a feeling that it does.
