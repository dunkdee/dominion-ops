## §1 — DOCTRINE AMENDMENT (resolves Engineering Standard v1.0 conflict)
Engineering Standard v1.0 restricts Dominion to a single active expansion vertical. Founder direction is multi-lane. Both are preserved by splitting lane activity into three tiers:

- **TIER A — MAINTAIN** (unlimited parallel): monitoring, health, audits, documentation, prep-only work, content generation held in review. No new external side effects, no new spend.
- **TIER B — PREPARE** (unlimited parallel): building, testing, staging, simulation, packet assembly, drafts. Reversible. No irreversible commitments.
- **TIER C — EXPAND** (**maximum ONE lane at a time**): irreversible or commercial action — paid acquisition spend, external publication, live money, filings, claimant contact, new provider contracts.

**Ruling:** all six lanes may run parallel in Tier A and B permanently. Tier C is held by exactly one lane until that lane clears the Revenue Proof Gate (§2), then Tier C passes to the next lane in the order set by the Founder.

**Current Tier C holder: Publishing/Digital (per Amendment 06 §1 — supersedes VoltEdge).** All other lanes are Tier A/B until Publishing/Digital clears §2.

## §2 — REVENUE PROOF GATE (new non-waivable gate)
A lane may not claim PRODUCTION-REVENUE status, receive additional spend, or pass Tier C forward until all five are VERIFIED with evidence:

1. **First transaction** — real customer, real money, transaction ID recorded.
2. **Delivery proven** — the customer received the good or service; tracking number, download log, or delivery receipt.
3. **Reconciliation** — money landed in the Dominion account; provider payout record matches the order record.
4. **Unit economics positive** — revenue minus COGS, shipping, provider fees, and refund reserve is a positive number (§4).
5. **Repeatability** — the same path completed a second time without manual rescue.

Until all five are VERIFIED, the lane's status is `REVENUE: UNPROVEN`. Infrastructure health, published pages, or listed products do not satisfy this gate. No agent may report a lane as profitable on projections.

## §3 — DEMAND LANE (the missing lane — new, governed)
Dominion has supply infrastructure and no governed demand infrastructure. This creates it.

**Contract:** the Demand Lane's only job is delivering qualified human traffic to a live offer and reporting cost per acquisition with evidence.

**Channel eligibility (Meta remains RESTRICTED_HOLD — not eligible):**
- **Tier 1 — organic, zero spend, no platform risk:** short-form video (TikTok/YouTube Shorts/Reels via approved accounts only), YouTube long-form, SEO/product-page content, email to owned list (subject to HOLD preflight).
- **Tier 2 — paid, requires Founder GO per channel with a stated daily cap:** Google Shopping / Performance Max, Microsoft Ads, YouTube ads.
- **Prohibited:** any Meta property, any workaround of a platform restriction, purchased traffic of unverified origin, any channel where Dominion lacks an authorized account in good standing.

**Governance:**
- Every channel starts at **daily cap = $0** and moves only by recorded Founder GO naming the channel, the cap, and the kill threshold.
- **Kill rule:** if cost per acquisition exceeds contribution margin per order for 3 consecutive days, the channel auto-pauses and reports BLOCKED. No agent may raise a cap to "give it time."
- Creative published only from verified live offers, verified prices, and verified stock. No expired promos, no unsupported shipping claims, no health claims outside the Dominion Healing compliance language.
- Publication still passes the review queue. This lane creates demand; it does not gain publishing authority.

## §4 — UNIT ECONOMICS GATE (VoltEdge and every future commerce lane)
No SKU may be advertised, promoted, or featured until its economics are VERIFIED:

`Contribution margin = sale price − supplier cost − shipping − payment fee − platform fee − refund reserve`

- Refund reserve default: 3% of sale price until real refund data exists.
- Any SKU with contribution margin **< 15%** is ineligible for paid acquisition.
- Any SKU with **negative** contribution margin is hidden, not promoted, not deleted (preserves history).
- Output required: a SKU economics table with a truth state per row. UNKNOWN supplier cost = ineligible, not assumed.

This runs **before** the Demand Lane spends a dollar. Advertising an unknown-margin SKU is prohibited.

## §5 — MEASUREMENT CONTRACT
Claims about performance require these, or the claim is UNKNOWN:
- **Traffic:** GA4 sessions by source/medium (property: Primal VoltEdge, G-VX7JFL1WZK).
- **Conversion:** orders and revenue from the Wix/provider record, not from an analytics estimate.
- **Cost:** actual platform spend from the ad account.
- **Derived:** CAC, contribution margin per order, and payback — each labeled VERIFIED or INFERRED.
- **Cadence:** one evidence-backed report per week to the Founder. Weekly report is a deliverable, not a dashboard glance.
- No blended or estimated attribution may be presented as VERIFIED.
