# Dominion Revenue Manifesto

## Purpose

Dominion exists to convert owned assets, governed intelligence, and repeatable operating systems into **verifiable profitable cash flow**.

The operating order is fixed:

1. **Cash flow**
2. **Systems**
3. **Scale**

Dominion will not confuse traffic, estimates, attention, projections, or agent output with earned revenue. Revenue is verified only when funds have posted, obligations are known, delivery is recorded, and the result survives reconciliation.

## The commercial thesis

Dominion does not depend on one platform, one product, or one agent. It builds a small number of disciplined revenue engines on shared infrastructure, proves each engine, and then replicates the controls across additional verticals.

The first revenue wave is:

1. **DominionHealing Wix Store**
2. **KDP Publishing**
3. **Analytics Services**, operating as the intelligence and service-product support track

Wix and KDP are the first owned-asset cash engines. Analytics converts permitted market signals and client-owned evidence into audits, benchmarks, and optimization services. It does not sell raw identifiable or health-sensitive records.

## The flywheel

```text
Permitted market signals and first-party evidence
→ validation, provenance, and classification
→ Wix and KDP product decisions
→ human-approved publication or change
→ first-party sales and performance measurement
→ documented lessons and reusable playbooks
→ analytics products and recurring optimization services
→ reinvest verified profit into the next controlled vertical
```

## Wave discipline

Dominion will not activate all verticals simultaneously. A wave may contain at most two primary revenue verticals. Supporting intelligence may run in shadow mode, but it may not create external effects without the required approval.

Wave 1 contains Wix Store and KDP Publishing. Analytics Services supports Wave 1 and prepares a third cash engine, but prospect contact, pricing, publication, and delivery remain human-gated.

## Wix Store doctrine

The Wix Store proves that Dominion can operate a real commerce loop:

- governed product catalog
- accurate descriptions and claims
- accessible storefront
- verified checkout
- recorded order and fulfillment state
- customer-service and refund obligations
- first-party analytics
- margin and conversion measurement

No catalog mutation, price change, health claim, shipping claim, supplier submission, or publication is autonomous.

## KDP Publishing doctrine

KDP converts owned intellectual property into a catalog of books and educational assets.

Every title requires:

- authorship and rights evidence
- originality review
- editorial and formatting checks
- cover and interior asset rights
- metadata and category review
- proof review
- pricing and royalty review
- Human Overseer publication approval
- post-launch sales and reader-feedback measurement

AI may assist drafting, editing, formatting, metadata preparation, and analysis. It may not invent authorship, citations, testimonials, credentials, or rights.

## Analytics Services doctrine

Dominion monetizes **analysis and operational value**, not raw sensitive data.

Approved products include:

- storefront growth-intelligence audits
- competitor and channel benchmarks
- content and product opportunity reports
- first-party measurement dashboards
- recurring optimization reports
- small read-only analytics systems

External estimates remain estimates. They cannot verify revenue, conversion, causation, or profitability. Client-owned data must be authorized, minimized, and handled under a defined purpose.

## Control doctrine

Every consequential action follows:

```text
proposal
→ evidence and provenance
→ schema validation
→ permissions and policy
→ independent review
→ Human Overseer approval when required
→ controlled execution
→ result verification
→ immutable evidence record
```

No agent may self-promote, expand permissions, conceal uncertainty, fabricate evidence, or convert an estimate into a factual business claim.

## Revenue truth

A large enterprise outcome is a strategic target, **not a forecast or guarantee**.

Dominion measures progress through:

- posted revenue
- gross profit
- contribution margin
- conversion rate
- refund and failure rate
- customer acquisition cost when applicable
- repeat purchase or catalog sell-through
- delivery quality
- cycle time
- verified learning retained by the system

The system earns the right to scale only after unit economics and operational reliability are demonstrated.

## Founder authority

Dewayne Singleton remains the Human Overseer and final decision-maker for publication, pricing, contracts, customer representations, sensitive-data use, money movement, production activation, and changes to Dominion's constitution.

The purpose of automation is not to remove human authority. It is to make disciplined execution faster, more consistent, more measurable, and easier to repeat.
