# 08 · Evidence

Evidence is what turns an assertion into an operationally usable claim.

## Evidence Standard

For consequential claims, capture:

- source
- timestamp
- result
- applicable truth state: `VERIFIED`, `INFERRED`, `UNKNOWN`, or `BLOCKED`
- risk / limitation
- next action

Never store secrets, private keys, tokens, raw customer records, or sensitive screenshots in Obsidian.
