# 15 · Founder Oversight

> [!governance] Founder control surface
> This page is a navigation and evidence template. It does not activate work, grant authority, or prove live runtime state.

## What the Founder can see for every mission

- objective and beneficiary
- facts, unknowns, assumptions, risks, and truth state
- lane owner, scope, conflict check, and bounded state
- required approval and independent review
- evidence references, verification gate, stop conditions, and rollback conditions
- Dominion memory destination and follow-up obligations

## Governed lane states

| Lane | State | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Intelligence | Governed reasoning only | Nemotron plans and challenges; it does not execute or self-approve. |
| Context and memory | Governed memory only | Metatron and Obsidian prepare sanitized context; no runtime activation. |
| Execution | Dormant by default | Conductor and specialists may prepare bounded work, simulations, and approved read-only checks only. |
| Verification | Read only | Independent reviewers collect evidence and report drift; they cannot alter production state. |
| Impact and revenue | Draft only | Operators may research and draft; no public distribution, customer contact, or unsupported revenue claim. |

## Protected holds

- TikTok: **DISABLED**
- Trading and Markov: **PAPER_ONLY**
- Wix: **RECORD_ONLY**
- Payments, publications, customer contact, credential changes, and production service changes: **NOT AUTHORIZED**

## Evidence standard

Use the governed schemas:

- governance/schemas/founder_mission_record.schema.json
- governance/schemas/founder_evidence_record.schema.json

Every consequential record must use VERIFIED, INFERRED, UNKNOWN, or BLOCKED, cite retrievable evidence, and exclude secrets, private keys, tokens, raw customer data, and raw environment files.
