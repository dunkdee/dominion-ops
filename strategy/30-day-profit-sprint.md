# Dominion 30-Day Profit Sprint

## Objective
Create measurable revenue within 30 days by concentrating execution on three lanes only:

1. Dominion Healing store offers
2. KDP and digital products
3. Traffic content that feeds those offers

No new verticals, agents, or paid tools are added during this sprint unless Dewayne gives explicit approval.

## Operating Rule
Every task must map to one of four outcomes:

- Publish an offer
- Generate qualified traffic
- Increase conversion
- Capture measurable learning

Anything else is backlog.

## Daily Execution Loop

1. Metatron loads current repository decisions and prior lessons.
2. Demand Miner identifies active problems, language, objections, and buying signals.
3. Opportunity Scorer ranks ideas by urgency, margin, speed, proof, and audience fit.
4. Offer Engineer produces one clear offer with price, promise, proof, delivery, and CTA.
5. Brand and Accuracy Review reject weak, misleading, or off-brand output.
6. Traffic Engine creates hooks, short-form posts, long-form content, email, and landing-page copy.
7. Human GO controls publication, spending, legal claims, and production changes.
8. Results are recorded and the winning lesson is promoted into Metatron.

## Week 1 — Revenue Foundation

- Select one flagship store offer and one digital/KDP offer.
- Confirm pricing, fulfillment, margins, claims, refund language, and checkout path.
- Produce one conversion landing page per offer.
- Create 30 short-form hooks and 7 core content pieces.
- Install basic tracking for visits, leads, checkout starts, sales, and source.
- Run end-to-end purchase and delivery tests.

Exit condition: both offers are buyable, measurable, mobile-ready, and tested.

## Week 2 — Traffic Launch

- Publish daily across the strongest available social channels.
- Repurpose each core asset into short video scripts, captions, email, and community posts.
- Use three hook families: pain, transformation, and proof.
- Build one lead magnet or low-friction entry offer.
- Collect objections, comments, click behavior, and search language.

Exit condition: traffic is reaching tracked pages every day and audience signals are being captured.

## Week 3 — Conversion Optimization

- Rank content and pages by click-through, lead rate, checkout-start rate, and sales.
- Kill weak hooks and duplicate the strongest angles.
- Rewrite headlines, CTA, proof, offer stack, and FAQ based on real objections.
- Add follow-up email or message sequence for non-buyers and abandoned checkouts where permitted.
- Create one bundle or upsell only after the core offer converts.

Exit condition: at least one repeatable traffic-to-offer path has measurable conversion data.

## Week 4 — Scale the Winner

- Concentrate output on the highest-converting audience, hook, channel, and offer.
- Increase content volume through structured repurposing, not lower quality.
- Document the winning workflow as a reusable skill.
- Automate only stable repeated steps.
- Produce the next 30-day forecast from actual conversion and traffic data.

Exit condition: Dominion has a documented, repeatable revenue loop and a clear scaling target.

## Daily Scoreboard

Track:

- Offers live
- Content published
- Qualified visits
- Leads captured
- Checkout starts
- Sales
- Gross revenue
- Refunds
- Conversion rate
- Revenue by traffic source
- Best hook
- Biggest objection
- Decision promoted to Metatron

## Stop Rules

Stop or revise a tactic when:

- It generates attention but no qualified clicks.
- It produces clicks but no meaningful offer engagement.
- It depends on unsupported claims.
- Fulfillment is unreliable.
- The cost or complexity violates the zero-paid sprint constraint.
- Results cannot be measured.

## Governance

Dewayne retains final authority. Agents may research, draft, critique, test, and recommend. They may not publish sensitive claims, spend money, change production infrastructure, or make irreversible decisions without human GO.

## Profit Reality

Strong profit inside 30 days is possible, but not guaranteed. The sprint is designed to maximize the probability by narrowing focus, shipping quickly, measuring real behavior, and scaling only verified winners.
